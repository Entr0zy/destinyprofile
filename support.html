Destiny Profile: Tarot Support

Something not working, or a question about a reading? Email entrozy@protonmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two days. It helps to say which phone you're on.

Common questions

The scanner got a card wrong. Pick the right card when it asks. The app learns your deck on your phone and gets better each time.

Where are my readings? Only on your phone. There's no account, so nothing is backed up by us. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

The camera won't open. Allow Destiny Profile: Tarot to use the camera in your phone's settings, then try again.
