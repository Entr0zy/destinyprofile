Destiny Profile: Tarot Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Destiny Profile: Tarot is made by ENTR0ZY. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android (package com.destinyprofile.app).

The short version

Destiny Profile: Tarot does not collect, store or share any of your data. There is no account and no sign-up. Readings are worked out on your phone, and nothing you enter leaves it.

What you enter

Your questions, readings, notes and settings are saved only on your device. We can't see them, and they are never sent to us or anyone else.

Camera and photos

If you choose to scan a physical spread, the app uses the camera, or a photo you pick, to recognise the cards. The photo is processed on your phone. A small copy may be kept on your device so the app can learn your deck. Photos are never uploaded.

Optional reading server

The app works fully offline. An advanced setting lets you connect it to a reading server that you run yourself. If you set one, your reading and question are sent only to that server. It is off by default, and we don't run one.

No tracking

Destiny Profile: Tarot has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Sharing

Readings you share go only where you send them, through your phone's own share sheet.

Children

Destiny Profile: Tarot does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a reading inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
